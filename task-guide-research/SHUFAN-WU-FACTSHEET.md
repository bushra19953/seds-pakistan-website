# Dr. Shufan Wu — Fact Sheet

Prepared 2026-10-06 for SEDS Pakistan induction use. Every claim below is paired with its source. Anything unverifiable is flagged as such.

## Identity (verified)

- **Name:** Shufan Wu (吴树范 — Chinese characters NOT verified; do not use without confirmation)
- **Title:** Chair Professor, School of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), Shanghai, China
- **Professional standing:** Full Member of the International Academy of Astronautics (IAA); Associate Fellow of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA)
- **Research leadership:** Director of the Smart Satellite Technology Centre (SSTC), SJTU School of Aeronautics and Astronautics — focus on smart micro/nano satellite technologies, space science, in-orbit technology demonstration, and practical satellite applications
- **Source:** https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Shufan-Wu-2 (his own ResearchGate profile text); https://www.zoominfo.com/p/Shufan-Wu/2299986149 (SSTC director role)

## Education (verified)

- Doctor of Engineering / PhD and MSc from the Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA)
- Postdoctoral work as a Humboldt Research Fellow at TU Braunschweig, Germany
- **Source:** https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Shufan-Wu-2 ; https://www.zoominfo.com/p/Shufan-Wu/2299986149

## Career (verified)

- 17 years in Europe: TU Braunschweig, TU Delft, Surrey University (UK), and the European Space Research and Technology Centre (ESTEC) of the European Space Agency — over a decade at ESA as Senior Aerospace Engineer
- Returned to China in June 2013; joined the Shanghai Engineering Center for Microsatellites (SECM), Chinese Academy of Sciences — served as Chief Engineer / Fellow Engineer, set up the CubeSat and microsatellite department
- Adjunct Professor, ShanghaiTech University (2015–2017)
- Directed China's first CubeSat mission (September 2015): three small satellites for flight tracking and ship tracking, launched via SECM
- **Source:** https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Shufan-Wu-2 ; http://www.satellitetoday.com/technology/2015/11/12/china-getting-in-on-smallsat-buzz/ ; https://www.zoominfo.com/p/Shufan-Wu/2299986149

## Research record (verified)

- 186 publications, ~1,700 citations on ResearchGate; fields: spacecraft attitude/control, satellite constellations, micro/nano satellites, space robotics, orbital mechanics
- Co-author on the ICUBE-Q lunar nanosatellite paper (Pakistan's lunar CubeSat on the Chang'E-6 mission) with Prof. Qamarul Islam and Prof. Khurram Khurshid of IST Pakistan
- **Source:** https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Shufan-Wu-2 ; https://iafastro.directory/iac/paper/id/90307/abstract-pdf/IAC-24,B4,8,3,x90307.brief.pdf?2024-03-28.11:12:57 (IAC 2024 paper ID 90307)
