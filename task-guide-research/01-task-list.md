# How Your Task List Works (Mission Control on Your Profile)

This is where you see every task assigned to you. Open your profile page on the
SEDS Pakistan website and scroll to the section titled **Mission Control**.

---

## 1. What the task list looks like

Your tasks appear as large cards, grouped under a divider banner that shows the
mission or workflow name (for example, the China Aerospace video mission).

Each task card shows, at a glance:

- **Directive number** — a badge like `DIRECTIVE # z20ZnLAP`, your task's ID.
- **Status badge** — colored pill showing where the task stands (see section 3).
- **Task title** — in bold capital letters, e.g. `PRINT PRODUCTION & MATERIAL PROCUREMENT`.
- **Attachments** — if the task has files or links, they appear as clickable
  chips labeled with the file name, or `ATTACHMENT 1`, `ATTACHMENT 2`.
- **Ends In** — a live countdown to your deadline.
- **Bounty** — how many points the task is worth, e.g. `4 PTS`.
- **Sync** — a percentage bar showing overall workflow progress.
- **Who gave it to you** — at the bottom of the card: the assignee (you), your
  role, and `Mission Command / HQ` as the issuer with the date it was issued.

At the top of the section you will see a badge like **3 ACTIVE DIRECTIVES**
telling you how many open tasks you have, plus a circular refresh button.

## 2. Filters: finding the right tasks

Above the cards there are two dropdown filters and a view toggle:

**Status filter** (funnel icon):
- `ALL STATUSES` — shows everything
- `STANDBY (TO DO)` — tasks you have not started yet
- `ACTIVE (IN PROGRESS)` — tasks you are currently working on
- `IN REVIEW` — tasks you submitted that are waiting for a reviewer
- `OVERDUE` — tasks past their deadline (shown in red)

**Project filter** (target icon): narrows the list to one project or mission.
Tasks with no project appear under `AUTONOMOUS DIRECTIVES`.

**View toggle**: switches between `Grid` and `List` layout. Same tasks, just
a different arrangement.

Tasks are sorted automatically: your current step comes first, then the rest
by nearest deadline.

## 3. What each status means

| Badge you see | Color | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Pending | Orange | The task is waiting for you to start it. |
| In Progress | Blue (spinning icon) | You are actively working on it. |
| Under Review | Yellow/amber (pulsing) | You submitted it; a reviewer is checking it. Locked until they decide. |
| Completed | Green (checkmark) | Approved and done. Completed tasks leave this list. |
| Overdue | Red (pulsing warning triangle) | The deadline passed and the task is not done. Act now. |

If a task is **your turn** (you are the assignee and it is the current step),
the card gets a highlighted blue border, a lightning-bolt icon, and a `YOU`
tag next to your name so you can spot it instantly. It also shows a
**TRANSMIT SUCCESS** quick-submit button right on the card.

## 4. Opening a task to see details

**Click anywhere on the task card** to expand it inline. Click again (or the
up-chevron button) to collapse it.

When expanded, the card reveals, top to bottom:

1. **Operational Briefing** — the `WHAT` section is always visible. `HOW`,
   `STANDARDS`, `RESOURCES`, and `VERIFICATION` are tap-to-expand rows
   (click the row to open each one).
2. **Tactical SITREP** — your working panel (see next section).
3. **Operational Mission Sequence** — the numbered steps of the whole mission,
   who is assigned to each, and which step is currently `ACTIVE`. Teammates
   with WhatsApp or email have quick-contact buttons here.
4. **Collaborative Mission Uplink** — the team chat for this mission.

There is also a **pencil icon** button on each card. Clicking it opens the same
task in a full detail dialog (tabs: overview, report, activity).

## 5. The SITREP panel: where you update and submit

Inside the expanded card, the **Tactical SITREP** panel is your workspace:

- **Status** — three buttons: `Standby`, `Active`, `Transmit`. Tap one to set
  where you are. `Transmit` means "I am done, send it for review."
- **Hours Logged** — a stepper (`-` / `+` buttons) to record time spent, in
  half-hour steps.
- **Execution Log** — a text box labeled `Detail outcomes, blockers, and
  deliverables...`. Write what you did here.
- **Submit button** — reads `TRANSMIT FOR REVIEW` when you picked Transmit,
  otherwise `SUBMIT SITREP`. Click it to save.

After you transmit, the panel locks and shows:
`Transmitted — awaiting review. Locked while the reviewer decides.`
A **Recall submission** button lets you pull it back to keep working.

If a reviewer asks for changes, their feedback appears in an amber
`Reviewer feedback` box at the top of the panel so you know exactly what to fix.

## 6. Empty state

If you have no open tasks, the section shows a checkmark icon with the text:

> **All Systems Nominal.**
> No active mission directives detected.

That means you are all caught up.
