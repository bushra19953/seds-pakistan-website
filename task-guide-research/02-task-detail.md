# Task Detail View — What You See and What Every Button Does

This is the expanded task card on your profile page (Profile → Mission Control → click a task to open it).

## 1. What you see when you open a task

**Top of the card (always visible):**
- **Directive number** — a short ID like "DIRECTIVE # z20ZnLAPd" (this is how the team refers to the task)
- **Status badge** — one of: STANDBY (to do), ACTIVE (in progress), IN REVIEW (you submitted it, waiting), OVERDUE (deadline passed), or Completed
- **Task title** in big letters
- **Resource chips** — clickable links the task gives you (Google Drive folders, GitHub repos, attachments). Click them to open the materials you need.

**Three info boxes:**
- **Ends In** — live countdown to your deadline
- **Bounty** — how many points you earn for completing it (e.g. "4 PTS")
- **Sync** — overall progress of the whole mission this task belongs to

**People section:**
- Your name with a **YOU** badge, your role, and your WhatsApp/email if saved
- Who assigned it ("Mission Command") and when it was issued

**When you expand the card (click the chevron), you also see:**

- **Operational Briefing** — the full instructions, split into sections. "What" is shown openly; click **How**, **Standards**, **Resources**, and **Verification** to expand each one. Read all of these before starting.
- **Tactical SITREP** — this is YOUR workspace: status buttons, hours, and the execution log (see buttons below)
- **Operational Mission Sequence** — the other steps in this mission, who is doing each one, and their status. Your current step is highlighted.
- **Collaborative Mission Uplink** — a team chat for this mission

## 2. Every button and what it does

### On the collapsed card (before you expand)
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **TRANSMIT SUCCESS** (big blue button, only shows when it is your turn) | One-click submit. Instantly marks the task as submitted for review with a default note. Use this only when the work is 100% done. |
| Chevron down/up | Expands or collapses the detail view |
| Pencil icon | Opens the full detail dialog |
| WhatsApp icon (green) | Lets a manager message the assignee about this task on WhatsApp |

### Inside the expanded SITREP panel
| Button / field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Standby / Active / Transmit** (three pills) | Sets the task status. **Standby** = not started. **Active** = you are working on it. **Transmit** = you are done and want it reviewed. |
| **Hours Logged** (− / + stepper) | Log how many hours you worked, in half-hour steps |
| **Execution Log** (big text box) | Write what you did, what the outcomes were, any blockers, and paste links to your deliverables. Placeholder says: "Detail outcomes, blockers, and deliverables..." |
| **SUBMIT SITREP** (green gradient button) | Saves your status, hours, and execution log WITHOUT submitting. Use this to save progress while you keep working. |
| **TRANSMIT FOR REVIEW** (same button, label changes when you pick the Transmit pill) | Submits the task for review. A reviewer will now check your work. |
| **Recall submission** (grey button, only visible after you transmit) | Takes your submission BACK so you can keep working. Status goes back to Active. |

### Status filter (top of the task list)
Filters your tasks: **ALL STATUSES**, **STANDBY (TO DO)**, **ACTIVE (IN PROGRESS)**, **IN REVIEW**, **OVERDUE**.

## 3. What happens after you click TRANSMIT

1. The task status changes to **submitted-for-review** (IN REVIEW badge)
2. You see a success toast: **"Mission Success Transmitted!"** (quick button) or **"Operational Log Saved."** (full form)
3. Confetti fires on screen (yes, really)
4. The SITREP panel **locks** — you cannot edit the log or hours anymore. It shows: *"Transmitted — awaiting review. Locked while the reviewer decides. Recall it to keep working on it."*
5. The task appears in your reviewer's validation queue (in THEIR profile) for approval

## 4. Can you un-submit? (Recall)

Yes. After transmitting, a **"Recall submission"** button appears in the locked panel. Clicking it:
- Sets the status back to **in-progress** (Active)
- Unlocks the execution log and hours so you can keep working
- Shows a toast: **"Submission recalled — back to Active."**
- The reviewer will no longer see it in their queue

Use this if you transmitted too early or need to fix something.

## 5. Where do you put your work / proof?

There is **no file upload button** on the task card. Instead:

1. **Execution Log** (the big text box) — write your report here: what you finished, what the results were, and **paste links** to your deliverables (Drive links, docs, videos, etc.)
2. **Deliverable files themselves** — upload them to the place the brief tells you to. Check the **Verification** section of the Operational Briefing: it tells you exactly what proof is required and where to put it (usually a Google Drive folder linked in the **Resources** chips at the top of the card).
3. **Hours Logged** — record your time so it counts toward your points.

Rule of thumb: the *files* go where the brief says (Drive), the *written report and links* go in the Execution Log, then hit **TRANSMIT FOR REVIEW**.
